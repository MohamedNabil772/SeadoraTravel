Fix: SuperAdmin locked out of admin user management; drop defunct BookingManager refs

Root cause: RBAC rework made SuperAdmin the top role, but UsersController still
gates on [Authorize(Roles="Admin")], so the seeded SuperAdmin got 403 on
/api/users and /api/users/roles, and the edit-user roles list came back
empty/stale.

- JwtTokenGenerator: emit an "Admin" role claim for SuperAdmin users so every
  legacy Roles="Admin" gate accepts the top role (one place, all callers).
- UsersView: remove hardcoded BookingManager (a role that no longer exists) from
  the stats card and badge helper; make them role-agnostic. Role checkboxes were
  already sourced dynamically from /api/auth/api/users/roles.
